Lecture Decks

TOPIC 0

Introduction & Logistics

Why parallelism, course overview, and the parallel-systems landscape.
TOPIC 1

Parallel Programming Models

Shared memory vs. message passing, the thread/process analogy, and PGAS, data-parallel, MapReduce & transactional memory.
TOPIC 2

Performance Analysis

Speedup, efficiency, scalability, Amdahl's & Gustafson's laws, profiling & tracing.
TOPIC 3

Message Passing (MPI)

Point-to-point & collective communication, communicators, derived datatypes.
TOPIC 4

Shared-Memory Programming (OpenMP)

SMPs & threads, pthreads, fork-join, work-sharing & schedules, data environment, reduction, sync constructs, tasks & SIMD — expanded for a systems course.
TOPIC 5

Vector Programming (SIMD)

Data-level parallelism, intrinsics, vectorization & optimization.
TOPIC 6

Cache & Memory Systems

Memory hierarchy & locality, caches, coherence (MESI), cache-friendly code, and a matrix-multiply tiling case study.
TOPIC 7

Parallel Architectures

Flynn's taxonomy, SIMD/MIMD, tightly- vs. loosely-coupled, UMA/NUMA, and a simple intro to cache coherence & memory consistency.
TOPIC 8 · GPU SESSION 1

The CUDA Programming Model

Host/device, kernels, the grid/block/thread hierarchy & indexing, memory transfers, naive matmul.
TOPIC 8 · GPU SESSION 2

Memory, Tiling & Optimization

Function qualifiers, warps & divergence, memory hierarchy, __syncthreads, tiled matmul, coalescing, bank conflicts, occupancy & unrolling.
TOPIC 9

Interconnection Networks

Topology, flow control, routing, deadlock avoidance & router microarchitecture.
TOPIC 10

MapReduce & Spark

The MapReduce model, Hadoop, and Spark for large-scale data-parallel processing.
TOPIC 11

Parallel I/O

Parallel file systems and MPI-IO for high-throughput data movement.
TOPIC 12

Fault Tolerance & Resilience

Checkpoint/restart & the Young/Daly interval, message logging, replication, ABFT, and resilience across MPI (ULFM), Spark & deep-learning training.
TOPIC 13 · ADVANCED

Parallel Deep Learning

Data / model / pipeline parallelism, 3D parallelism, and model compression (pruning, quantization).
TOPIC 13 · ADVANCED

HP (Multi-)Linear Algebra

Dense tensor contraction (TBLIS, CTF, cuTENSOR, GETT/Linnea) and sparse tensors — formats, MTTKRP/TTM kernels, and CP/Tucker decompositions.
TOPIC 13 · ADVANCED

Other GPUs & AI Accelerators

AMD & Intel GPUs, Google TPU, and Tenstorrent (Tensix: NoC, RISC-V, circular buffers, tiles) with single- & multi-core matmul labs.
